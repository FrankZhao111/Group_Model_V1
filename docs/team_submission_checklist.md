# Team submission checklist

This is a checklist of requirements, not AI-written slide content. The team must interpret the outputs and write the submission itself.

- Title slide: team name and members, repository link, and the standalone script path `run_model.py`.
- Business question and the person making the product decision; distinguish information emphasis from a validated clinical referral decision.
- Data: CDC NHANES, 2007-2008, one row per actual participant, declared 29-person demonstration sample from a larger pool, five inputs, no clustering outcome, zero imputation.
- Split: fewer than 30 means use all 29 to train. Clearly mark held-out evaluation, a test elbow, and train/test agreement as unavailable under that exception.
- Specification: K-means, category encoding, four continuous fields standardized, category weight, candidate k range, selected k and the close alternative.
- Fit: one-group baseline versus v1 using the training elbow and combined spread. Use chart files generated by running the code yourselves. Do not call the training SSE reduction medical accuracy or risk reduction.
- Interpretation: group counts and means, small-group and sensitivity findings, and why the labels express relative risk-factor burden rather than measured future quality-of-life loss.
- Decision: run `predict.py` on a realistic new user and explain how saved scaling, encoding, and center distances determine the information package.
- Business units: generic versus enhanced navigation-time assumptions, future-mix assumption, and baseline versus v1 minutes. Label the values as hypothetical.
- Deliverable: one PDF containing the title slide and two business slides written by the team. Every team member should be able to explain the numbers they include.

`outputs/RESULTS.md` and the CSV/JSON files provide the technical numbers. They are not the team's presentation narrative. App integration is not required for this assignment.
